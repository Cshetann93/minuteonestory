Vikram checked his bike mirror for the fourth time.
A white Swift was tailing him through the dark service lanes.
Every turn he made, the car made too.
Near a closed petrol pump, Vikram flicked his right indicator.
He waited for an oncoming auto to pass.
Then he cut hard into the narrow left alley instead.
The Swift didn't turn.
It rolled into the empty crossroads and stopped dead.
Vikram pulled over thirty meters ahead, heart racing.
The car’s hazard lights started blinking in the dark.
The driver stepped out into the glow of Vikram's headlight.
In his hand was a worn brown leather wallet.
He held it up, tapping the driving license inside.
Vikram felt his kurta pocket.
The zip was undone. It was completely empty.
