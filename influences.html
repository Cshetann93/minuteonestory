Influences & Recommendations

A curated collection of films, books, essays, and creators that deeply understand tension, atmosphere, structure, and emotional storytelling.

Films

  • Zodiac — masterclass in procedural tension and atmosphere.
  • Prisoners — emotional suspense built through moral pressure.
  • No Country For Old Men — minimal dialogue and relentless inevitability.
  • Parasite — tonal control and visual storytelling perfection.

Books

  • Story by Robert McKee — structure, turning points, and emotional movement.
  • Save The Cat — accessible screenplay rhythm and pacing.
  • Into The Woods — advanced dramatic structure analysis.

YouTube & Essays

  • Every Frame A Painting — visual storytelling and cinematic language.
  • Lessons from the Screenplay — screenplay mechanics through film analysis.
  • StudioBinder — filmmaking craft, blocking, lighting, and writing insights.
